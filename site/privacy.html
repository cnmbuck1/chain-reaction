Privacy policy

Chain Reaction, by Cnmbuck Studios · Last updated 7 November 2026

Chain Reaction is a logic puzzle game. The app is free and shows ads, which you can turn off with a one-time purchase. There are no accounts and no sign-in. We don't collect anything about you ourselves and never sell anything.

What stays on your device

Your solved levels, stars, coins, daily puzzle streak, play statistics and settings are saved on your device only. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them, and Settings → Progress → Reset clears your progress at any time.

Sharing your daily result

If you tap Share on a daily puzzle result, the game opens your phone's share sheet with a short text of your result. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch for coins, and, unless you buy Remove ads, an occasional full-screen ad between levels. To show and measure ads, Google collects information such as your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone only if you allow it when the app asks to track; on Android you can reset or delete it in your phone's Google settings), IP address, rough location derived from it, device type and ad interactions. If you don't allow tracking, you still see ads, just less personalised ones. Players in the EU, UK and similar regions are asked for consent first and can change their choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Coins and Remove ads are bought through Apple (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android). We don't see your payment details; the store tells the app only that the purchase was made. Remove ads can be restored on a new device with Shop → Restore purchases.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
