How to play

  • Tap switches to set the ball's path, then tap Run to send the ball down the board.
  • Get the ball to the goal. Three stars need the fewest changes.
  • Stuck? A hint flips one correct switch for you.
  • A new daily puzzle opens every day once you've solved the first levels.

Common questions

Where did my progress go? Progress is saved on your device. It isn't shared between devices, and deleting the app deletes it.

I bought Remove ads on another phone. Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases.
